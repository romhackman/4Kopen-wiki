4Kopen

An Open 4K UHD Video Development Platform

STMicroelectronics | 2019

What is 4Kopen?

4Kopen is an open-source hardware development platform designed for Ultra HD video applications. In 2019, it featured STMicroelectronics' STiH418 multimedia processor.

Key Features

  • Processor: Quad-core ARM Cortex-A9, up to 1.2 GHz
  • Video output: HDMI 2.0, up to 4K at 60 fps
  • Memory: 2 GB DDR3 RAM
  • Connectivity: Gigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi and USB 3.0
  • Software: Linux-based development environments and GStreamer support

Why is it interesting?

4Kopen provides developers with a flexible platform for testing and building high-quality video applications. Its open hardware design and connectivity options make it useful for multimedia experimentation.
